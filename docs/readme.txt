************************************************************************************************
2. Introduction
************************************************************************************************

You are a pizza deliverer on Frog City. Your work is to deliver pizzas as fast as you can. But
there are problems. The city is full of crazy frog drivers, that run over everything that cross their path. There are thieves running along the sidewalks. Be careful with these guys. You can lose your pizza and money.


************************************************************************************************
3. Gameplay
************************************************************************************************

The objective is to deliver as many pizzas as you can. To get the pizza, you only need to touch the pizzaria that is located on the botoom of the screen. Then you deliver the pizza where the ballon "HERE !" appears.

Game Rules:

- For each 500 points you'll gain a life.

- For each pizza you deliver successfully, you'll gain 15 points. 

- For each pizza you deliver, the car speed you increase 2%.

- You have only 20 seconds to deliver the pizza. If you let the time run out you'll lose 30 points. 

- If a thief steal your pizza, you'll lose 15 points, and the timer will not be reseted.

- If you were ran over by a car you'll lose a life, but not points.


************************************************************************************************
4. Controls
************************************************************************************************

The Xfrog controls are pretty simple. Use the keyboard arrows to move, and "SPACE" key to deliver the pizza. If you want to pause the game, just press "ESC", and let the game stay on the menu. To return to the game chosse the "RETURN" option. To start a new game select "NEW GAME".


************************************************************************************************
5. Items
************************************************************************************************

There are 5 items on Xfrog. They are divided in 2 groups: Food and Specials.

With the Food item you can earn money by picking up them:

- Flour:    10 points
- Tomatoes: 20 points
- Cheese:   30 points


The Specials items can change the gameplay:

- Speed: By picking up this item, you'll run 3x faster than the normal speed.

- Stop Clock: This clock can stop the world. Only you can move around. The cars, the time, and the thieves you STOP. This item is rare.


************************************************************************************************
6. Extra Info
************************************************************************************************

Xfrog works on a fake 3D perspective. I've used the Painter's algorithm to draw the screen. So you can stay behind and in front of the cars. This gives you the possibility to stay in the middle of the street without problems.

If you want you can press ALT+TAB in the middle of game. Then you can leave the game minimized. The game will free 90% of the CPU. I don't know if this feature will work on all video cards. So be warned.
